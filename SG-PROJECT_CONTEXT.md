# Steppe Gut — Project Context

## Overview
Steppe Gut is currently developing its brand identity while simultaneously completing two key regulatory workstreams:
- Import permit acquisition
- Thai FDA registration

The website is being developed before the commercial launch of the product and therefore has two equally important objectives.

---

## Primary Objectives

### 1. Educate the Market
Fermented mare's milk is largely unfamiliar to Thai consumers. The website should clearly explain:
- What fermented mare's milk is
- Why it is different
- How it is produced
- Why it benefits consumers
- How it should be consumed

Education should be integrated naturally throughout the user journey rather than isolated on a single page.

### 2. Build Trust
The website should establish credibility before customers are ready to purchase. Throughout the website, reinforce trust through:
- Mongolian sourcing
- Manufacturing information
- Product quality
- Scientific explanation
- Regulatory progress where appropriate
- Premium presentation
- Professional UX
- Clear communication

The website should make visitors feel that Steppe Gut is a legitimate premium wellness brand entering the Thai market.

---

## Brand Story
Steppe Gut is proud of its Mongolian origin. Mongolia should become a central part of the brand narrative rather than a minor detail.

The About page should communicate:
- The Mongolian steppe
- Traditional production
- Local sourcing
- Authenticity
- Heritage
- Product provenance

This story should also be reinforced subtly throughout the rest of the website where appropriate.

---

## Website Goals
The website should prioritise:
1. Education
2. Trust
3. Brand storytelling
4. Product understanding
5. Conversion

Every page should contribute to at least one of these objectives.

---

## User Journey
Assume most visitors have never heard of fermented mare's milk. The website should progressively answer the following questions:

1. **What is this product?**
2. **Why does it exist?**
3. **Why should I trust it?**
4. **Why is Mongolia important?**
5. **How does it work?**
6. **Why is it different from alternatives?**
7. **Why should I buy it?**
