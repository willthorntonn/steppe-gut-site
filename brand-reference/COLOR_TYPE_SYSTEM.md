## Colour palette

### Core colours (all variations)

| Name | Role | Hex | RGB | CMYK |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forest | Primary | `#2F3E2F` | 47, 62, 47 | 71, 53, 75, 55 |
| Cream | Secondary | `#F5F1E9` | 245, 241, 233 | 2, 4, 7, 0 |
| Gold | Tertiary | `#D4AF37` | 212, 175, 55 | 18, 29, 96, 0 |
| Sage | Quaternary | `#7D9D75` | 125, 157, 117 | 54, 24, 64, 3 |
| Earth | Tertiary (muted variation only) | `#442D1C` | 68, 45, 28 | 49, 68, 82, 63 |

### ✅ Active website palette: Vibrant / Gold

Forest · Cream · Gold · Sage — `design-tokens.js` sets `activePalette: 'vibrant'`.

Gold used **sparingly, as accent, not fill** (per `SG-PROJECT_SPEC.md`).

Earth/Muted is documented brand data but **not used** on the website (reserved for packaging / a possible future sub-brand).

## Typography

| Role | Typeface | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Primary — English (display/headings, `STEPPE GUT` wordmark) | **EB Garamond** (serif) | 400 · 600 · 700 |
| Secondary — English (body/UI) | **Inter** (sans-serif) | 400 · 600 · 700 |
| Primary — Thai | **Noto Sans Thai** | 300 · 400 · 700 |

Body sizes documented: 16px and 12px (Regular 400). Heading sizes are not specified in the source.
